### 🔭 What I'm working on

- **AI safety research** at the BU Security Lab: indirect prompt injection and jailbreaks in LLM chat assistants caused by unsanitised context passed between models and tools.
- **Multimodal fraud detection** at the BU Cyber Forensics Lab: fusing transaction, network, face, voice and document signals to catch synthetic identity fraud, plus an OSINT dataset linking OFAC-sanctioned crypto addresses to real-world networks.
- **Onboard live video for the Argo sounding rocket** with the BU Rocket Propulsion Group: 1.2 GHz downlink under 2 W, high-gain Yagi ground station, ~98,000 ft slant range, $170 COTS budget.
